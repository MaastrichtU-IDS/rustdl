## The whelk-rs comparison — key findings (`docs/whelk-rs-comparison-2026-07-08.md`)

- **rustdl = ELK-kernel + hypertableau-wedge + Konclude-style orchestrator.** whelk-rs is a
  minimal EL-only ELK; rustdl extends the same fixpoint (EL⁺⁺ functional-merge + super-EL
  reductions: NomKey/MaxKey/ForallKey/DKey/⊤⊑C/∃R.⊤) and adds a tableau for full SROIQ.
- **Performance:** rustdl faster on the large majority of EL onts (ORE median 1.64×,
  BioPortal 2.20×); **whelk faster only on the largest** (2–5×, structure-dependent — e.g.
  10248 ~5×, 11739 ~1.2×; go-basic 1.8×). Root cause = rustdl's per-event EL⁺⁺ guard
  overhead (a constant factor, not algorithmic).
- **whelk-rs is itself unsound** on a defined-class-∃-over-subproperty pattern
  (`ore_ont_3406/13224`) — Konclude-confirmed. **LESSON: adjudicate every rustdl/whelk
  disagreement against an independent Konclude oracle; do not treat "the complete reasoner"
  as ground truth.** (ROBOT convert→OWX; `konclude classification`; parse SubClassOf/Equiv.)
- **vs HermiT/Konclude:** same three families — saturation (whelk, rustdl-kernel) / (hyper)tableau
  (HermiT, rustdl-wedge) / hybrid (Konclude, rustdl). Konclude is the mature speed leader
  (2.2×–809× on real SROIQ); rustdl shares its design point but is a younger implementation.
